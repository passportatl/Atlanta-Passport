ATLANTA PASSPORT — TOURIST-FIRST UX REFINEMENT

IMPORTANT STRATEGIC SHIFT

The site should now prioritize TOURISTS first.

Businesses are important, but they should feel secondary and intentional.

Right now the “Apply” button in the header makes the experience feel too business-facing too early.

The homepage should feel like:
a colorful Atlanta discovery guide for visitors.

Businesses should discover partnership opportunities naturally throughout the experience instead of being hit with sales immediately.

“Platform looking for business partners.”

“Fun Atlanta discovery guide tourists actually want to use.”

“I want my business inside this.”

“I want to explore this city.”
