BRAND DIRECTION UPDATE

We are shifting the Atlanta Passport brand identity into something:
More playful
More iconic
More Atlanta
More memorable
More culturally recognizable

The brand should still feel curated and premium, BUT with stronger personality and visual identity.

CORE BRAND INSPIRATION:
Classic Waffle House signage energy
Retro Atlanta roadside culture
Southern Americana
Neighborhood discovery
Vintage travel guides
Modern playful editorial design

IMPORTANT:
DO NOT directly copy Waffle House branding or logos.
Instead:
Take inspiration from:
Block typography
Bold yellow signage
Retro roadside Americana
Simple iconic shapes
Playful familiarity

The goal is:
Instantly recognizable Atlanta energy.

NEW BRAND PERSONALITY

The site should feel:
Colorful
Fun
Warm
Local
Playful
Confident
