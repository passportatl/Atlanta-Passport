DEEP MOBILE BRAND + UX REFINEMENT FOR ATLANTA PASSPORT

The current site is strong and moving in the right direction. Now refine it from “good MVP” into a more iconic, collectible, playful Atlanta cultural guide.

Core tagline:
“The unofficial guide to the real Atlanta.”

Keep the Waffle-House-inspired yellow block logo feel, but make sure it remains original and not a direct copy.

IMPORTANT:
Do not use actual Waffle House, Coca-Cola, FIFA, World Cup, Hawks, Braves, Falcons, Atlanta United, or team logos.
Use only original design, inspired colors, and local Atlanta energy.

OVERALL GOAL

Make the site feel like:
A collectible Atlanta city guide
A playful mobile web-app
A local cultural product
A travel passport
A street-level Atlanta discovery game
A brand people would want as a sticker, tote, booklet, or hat

NOT:
A generic startup landing page
A boring directory
A corporate tourism site
A sports fan page
A Yelp clone

PRIMARY DESIGN FIXES

1. Improve typography hierarchy.
2. Add more texture, stickers, stamps, and tactile detail.
3. Make the hero feel more layered and energetic.
4. Tighten vertical spacing on mobile.
5. Unify the button system.
6. Add real Atlanta photography.
7. Make routes/passport collections more prominent.
8. Make the tourist experience feel like a discovery product.

TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Right now there are too many type styles competing.

Create strict typography rules:

A. Editorial Serif
Use for:
- Hero headline
- Emotional section headlines

- Manifesto copy

B. Retro Block Font
Use for:
- Logo
- Badges
- Buttons
- Stickers
- Labels
- Navigation accents

C. Clean Sans
Use for:
- Body copy
- Card descriptions
- Forms
- Supporting text

Do not introduce additional font styles.

Make sure:
- Headlines feel bold and editorial.
- Badges/buttons feel playful.
- Body copy feels clean and readable.

MOBILE SPACING FIXES

Tighten vertical spacing across mobile.

Reduce excessive:
- top padding between sections
- bottom padding after hero
- large blank gaps between cards
- overly tall empty areas

Mobile should feel like constant discovery.

Use:
section padding mobile: 56px top/bottom max
hero padding mobile: 48px top / 56px bottom
card gap mobile: 20px to 28px
headline-to-body gap: 18px to 24px

Avoid giant empty scroll zones.

Do not mix random orange, green, and SaaS-style button treatments unless they follow this same system.
