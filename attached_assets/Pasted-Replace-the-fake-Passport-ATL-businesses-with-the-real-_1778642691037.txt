BUSINESS STAMP LOGIC

When a visitor scans a business QR code:
1. Add that business stamp to their passport.
2. Also count progress toward that neighborhood stamp.
3. Once they collect at least 1 stamp in a neighborhood, show the neighborhood stamp as “started.”
4. Once they collect 3 stamps in that neighborhood, show the neighborhood stamp as “earned.”
5. Once they collect 5 stamps in that neighborhood, unlock that neighborhood’s secret route.

If a neighborhood has fewer than 5 listed businesses, unlock its secret route after all listed businesses are collected.
